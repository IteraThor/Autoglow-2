    Cameras
    About streaming: quality, frame rate, USB cameras

    AutoGlow streams the cameras to every device in the network: TVs, tablets, OBS. Autodarts cameras come from the Autodarts Board Manager, so the same camera detects darts and shows the game; AutoGlow fetches each one once for all screens. They send full-size pictures (about 3.5 MB/s per camera at 15 fps); use 10 fps if the Wi-Fi struggles. A USB camera can only be used by one program at a time; the quality setting applies to those.
